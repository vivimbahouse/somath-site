What to expect on your first visit

Most first-time visitors come for a free 60-minute evaluation. Here is how it works, start to finish.

  1. You are greeted at the door

    A director or one of our senior teachers meets you at the front entrance. We keep the group small so nobody is left waiting or wondering where to go.

  2. A short conversation with the parent

    About ten minutes. We ask about your child's current school, what they are working on, what concerns you as a parent, and what you are hoping for. No sales pitch — just a real conversation.

  3. A 45-minute diagnostic with your child

    Your child works with one of our instructors on a written diagnostic calibrated to their grade level. This is not a test they can pass or fail — it is a tool that tells us exactly where their understanding is solid and where the gaps are.

  4. A written summary within 48 hours

    You get a written diagnostic report by email within two business days. It covers what your child does well, where they need work, and what class (if any) fits them best — including our honest recommendation if we think you should consider a different program.

  5. No pressure to enroll

    Genuinely. Many families use the free evaluation as a second opinion and go back to their existing tutor or school with clearer next steps. We are here when you are ready — or not.

What we teach at 226 West 79th Street

Every class we run happens in our Upper West Side studio, in small groups of up to six students. Here is what's offered at this location.

  • Little Newtons — grades 1–3. Foundations of arithmetic, number sense, and problem-solving.
  • Kid Einsteins — grades 4–5. Fractions, decimals, ratios, pre-algebra readiness.
  • Young Fermats Pre-Algebra — grades 6–7. The bridge year to high-school algebra.
  • Algebra Ignite — grades 8–9. Regents and honors Algebra 1.
  • Geometry — grades 9–10. Regents and honors Geometry.
  • Algebra II — grades 10–11. Advanced algebra and functions.
  • Pre-Calculus — grades 10–11. AP-aligned pre-calc curriculum.
  • AP Calculus AB/BC — grades 11–12. Both AP tracks with exam prep.
  • AP Statistics — grades 11–12. Full AP Stats curriculum.
  • SAT Math — grades 10–12. Focused test prep.
  • SHSAT Prep — grades 7–8. For Stuyvesant, Bronx Science, Brooklyn Tech.

Frequently asked questions about visiting

Where exactly is School of Math located?

SOMATH is at 226 West 79th Street, 1st Floor, New York, NY 10024 — on the Upper West Side, half a block west of Broadway and steps from the 79th Street subway station on the 1 train.

What subway trains stop nearest to SOMATH?

The 1 train stops at 79th & Broadway — a 2-minute walk. The B and C trains stop at 81st & Central Park West — a 5-minute walk. The 2 and 3 express trains at 72nd & Broadway are a 10-minute walk. The M79 crosstown bus and M104 downtown bus both stop within one block.

Is there parking near SOMATH?

Metered street parking is available on West 79th Street and Broadway (2-hour limit weekdays, 8 AM – 7 PM). The Icon Parking garage at 200 W 79th St and the LAZ garage at 201 W 75th St both offer daily rates ($25–$40). Most Upper West Side families walk or take the subway.

What are your hours?

Monday through Friday from 3:00 PM to 10:00 PM (aligned with the after-school schedule), Saturday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Sunday from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Free evaluations are typically booked outside class time — weekday mornings or weekend afternoons work best.

Do I need an appointment to visit?

For a tour or a free evaluation, yes — please book online so we can make sure a director is available to walk you through the space. For quick questions or to drop off paperwork, feel free to stop by during program hours.

Is the building accessible?

SOMATH is on the ground floor of 226 W 79th St. There is one step at the entrance; contact us at (646) 668-6151 in advance if you need step-free access and we will arrange it.

Can I visit before enrolling?

Yes. Every family who enrolls at SOMATH starts with a free 60-minute evaluation — a diagnostic session for your child plus a chance for you to see the classroom, meet a director, and get a written recommendation within 48 hours. There is no pressure to enroll after the evaluation.

Ready to come by?

Book a free 60-minute evaluation online, or call us during program hours. We answer the phone.
