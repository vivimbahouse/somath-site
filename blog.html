Difference Quotient of f(x) = x³ at x = 2 (Stewart Ch.1 Q2)

Expand (2 + h)³ with the binomial expansion, subtract f(2) = 8, cancel the 8s, and divide each term by h. The simplified answer is h² + 6h + 12 — and as h → 0 it becomes f′(2) = 12. The algebraic foundation of every limit definition of the derivative.
