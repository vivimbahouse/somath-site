25 Practice Questions

Natural numbers, whole numbers, and integers on the number line (Q1–Q8)
Q1

Give an example of a number that is a whole number but NOT a natural number. Explain why.

Show answer & explanation

0

Why. Natural numbers start at 1 (N = {1, 2, 3, ...}). Whole numbers start at 0 (W = {0, 1, 2, 3, ...}). So 0 is the only whole number that is not a natural number.

Q2

Write the next three natural numbers after 47.

Show answer & explanation

48, 49, 50

Why. Natural numbers are the counting numbers. Just add 1 each time: 47 + 1 = 48, 48 + 1 = 49, 49 + 1 = 50.

Q3

On a number line, which is farther to the LEFT: −8 or −3?

Show answer & explanation

−8

Why. On a number line, negatives go left of 0. The bigger the negative sign, the farther left. −8 is 8 units left of 0; −3 is only 3 units left. So −8 is farther left.

Q4

Order these integers from LEAST to GREATEST: 5, −7, 0, −2, 3, −10.

Show answer & explanation

−10, −7, −2, 0, 3, 5

Why. Line them up on the number line. The most negative is smallest. −10 < −7 < −2 < 0 < 3 < 5.

Q5

Fill in the blank with < or >: −6 ___ −9.

Show answer & explanation

>

Why. On the number line, −6 is to the RIGHT of −9 (closer to 0), so −6 is greater than −9. −6 > −9.

Q6

The temperature at 6 a.m. was −4 degrees C. By noon, it rose 11 degrees. What is the temperature at noon?

Show answer & explanation

7 degrees C

Why. Start at −4 and move 11 units to the right on the number line: −4 + 11 = 7. So the temperature at noon is 7 degrees C.

Q7

A submarine is at −180 feet (180 feet below sea level). It descends another 65 feet. What is its new depth as an integer?

Show answer & explanation

−245 feet

Why. Descending means going more negative. −180 + (−65) = −245. The submarine is at −245 feet.

Q8

Sara owes her brother $12 (write it as an integer). She then earns $20 and pays her brother back in full. How much money does she have left?

Show answer & explanation

$8

Why. Owing $12 = −12. She earns $20 (+20), so −12 + 20 = 8. She has $8 left.

Absolute value (Q9–Q14)
Q9

What is |−15|?

Show answer & explanation

15

Why. Absolute value is the distance from 0 on the number line, and distance is always positive. |−15| = 15.

Q10

What is |7| + |−4|?

Show answer & explanation

11

Why. |7| = 7 and |−4| = 4 (distance is always positive). 7 + 4 = 11.

Q11

Solve: |−9| − |6|.

Show answer & explanation

3

Why. |−9| = 9 and |6| = 6. Then 9 − 6 = 3.

Q12

Find two different integers whose absolute value is 12.

Show answer & explanation

12 and −12

Why. |x| = 12 means x is 12 units from 0. Two numbers fit: 12 (right of 0) and −12 (left of 0).

Q13

A hiker walked 4 miles east, then 7 miles west from her starting point. Use absolute value to find the TOTAL distance she walked.

Show answer & explanation

11 miles

Why. Distance is always positive. Total distance = |4| + |−7| = 4 + 7 = 11 miles. (Her position changed, but she still walked 11 miles.)

Q14

Which is larger: |−20| or |15|? Explain briefly.

Show answer & explanation

|−20| is larger

Why. |−20| = 20 and |15| = 15. Since 20 > 15, |−20| is larger. Absolute value ignores the sign — only the distance from 0 matters.

Even, odd, and divisibility rules (Q15–Q19)
Q15

Is 246 even or odd? How do you know without dividing?

Show answer & explanation

Even

Why. Look at the last digit: 6. If the last digit is 0, 2, 4, 6, or 8, the number is even. So 246 is even.

Q16

Is 1,584 divisible by 3? Show your work using the divisibility rule.

Show answer & explanation

Yes

Why. Rule: a number is divisible by 3 if the sum of its digits is divisible by 3. 1 + 5 + 8 + 4 = 18. 18 is divisible by 3 (18 ÷ 3 = 6), so 1,584 is divisible by 3.

Q17

Is 4,725 divisible by 5? By 10? Explain each.

Show answer & explanation

By 5: yes. By 10: no.

Why. Rule for 5: last digit must be 0 or 5. 4,725 ends in 5, so YES divisible by 5. Rule for 10: last digit must be 0. 4,725 ends in 5, not 0, so NOT divisible by 10.

Q18

Find the smallest 3-digit number that is divisible by BOTH 2 AND 3.

Show answer & explanation

102

Why. Divisible by 2 AND 3 means divisible by 6. Smallest 3-digit number is 100, but 100 ÷ 6 is not whole. Try 102: 102 ÷ 2 = 51 (yes, even) and 1 + 0 + 2 = 3, divisible by 3. So 102 works.

Q19

A teacher has 96 students and wants to make equal teams. Can she make teams of 4 with no one left over? Show the divisibility check.

Show answer & explanation

Yes

Why. Rule for 4: the LAST TWO digits must form a number divisible by 4. Last two digits of 96 = 96. 96 ÷ 4 = 24 exactly, so yes — 96 students split into 24 teams of 4.

Classifying numbers with the N ⊂ W ⊂ Z Venn diagram (Q20–Q22)
Q20

Classify the number −3: is it a natural number, whole number, integer? (Check all that apply.)

Show answer & explanation

Integer only

Why. Natural numbers start at 1 (positive only). Whole numbers start at 0 (no negatives). Integers include negatives. So −3 is an integer, but not natural or whole.

Q21

True or false: Every whole number is an integer. Explain.

Show answer & explanation

True

Why. Whole numbers W = {0, 1, 2, 3, ...}. Integers Z include all whole numbers AND their negatives. So every whole number fits inside the set of integers. From the Venn diagram: N is inside W, W is inside Z.

Q22

Give a number that is an integer but NOT a whole number.

Show answer & explanation

Any negative integer (for example, −5)

Why. Whole numbers are 0, 1, 2, 3, ... (no negatives). Integers include the negatives. So any negative integer like −5, −1, −100 works.

Word problems mixing integers and absolute value (Q23–Q25)
Q23

Marcus withdraws $25 from his bank account three times. Write this as an integer expression and find the total change to his balance.

Show answer & explanation

−75

Why. Each withdrawal is −25. Three withdrawals: 3 × (−25) = −75. His balance goes down by $75, so the change is −75.

Q24

The elevator starts at floor 0 (ground floor). It goes UP 8 floors, then DOWN 3 floors, then DOWN 6 more. What floor is it on now? Write it as an integer.

Show answer & explanation

−1

Why. Start at 0. +8 = 8. Then 8 − 3 = 5. Then 5 − 6 = −1. The elevator is at floor −1 (one basement level).

Q25

On a math test, Ana's score changed by these amounts from question to question: +4, −3, +6, −8, +2. What is the NET change in her score, and what is the TOTAL DISTANCE her score moved (use absolute value)?

Show answer & explanation

Net change = +1. Total distance = 23.

Why. Net change: add signed values: 4 + (−3) + 6 + (−8) + 2 = 1. Total distance: use absolute values (distance is always positive): |4| + |−3| + |6| + |−8| + |2| = 4 + 3 + 6 + 8 + 2 = 23. This is the classic "position vs. path length" distinction — the same idea shows up in physics as displacement vs. distance.
