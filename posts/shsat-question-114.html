SHSAT question 114 – Constant of Proportionality (Ice Cream Problem)

Question

SHSAT question 114 -

An ice cream shop sells 16 cups of ice cream for $48 and 4 cups for $12. There is a proportional relationship between the number of cups of ice cream and the cost.

What is the constant of proportionality?

E. 3 cups per dollar
F. 3 dollars per cup
G. 4 cups per dollar
H. 4 dollars per cup

Step-by-Step Solution (Proportional Relationships)

To find the constant of proportionality, we calculate the unit rate (cost per cup).

48 ÷ 16 = 3
12 ÷ 4 = 3

The cost is consistent, confirming a proportional relationship.

👉 Constant of proportionality = 3 dollars per cup

Final Answer

F. 3 dollars per cup
