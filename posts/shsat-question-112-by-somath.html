Purple Paint Mixture Problem (Proportions)

Question:

The table below shows the number of cups of red paint and blue paint used to make a purple paint mixture.

Cups of Red Paint Cups of Blue Paint
1 1.5
4 6
11 y

Based on the relationship between red and blue paint, what is the value of y?

A) 10.5
B) 13
C) 16.5
D) 24

Step-by-Step Solution

This is a proportional relationship.

From the table:

When red = 1, blue = 1.5

So the ratio is constant: Blue = 1.5 × Red

For 11 cups of red paint: y = 1.5 × 11 = 16.5

Final Answer: 16.5

Why This Matters

This type of problem builds essential skills in:

  • Ratios and proportions
  • Algebraic thinking
  • Real-world math applications
