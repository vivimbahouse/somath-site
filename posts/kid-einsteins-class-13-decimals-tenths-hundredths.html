Understanding tenths

A tenth is one of 10 equal parts of a whole. To write a fraction with 10 on the bottom as a decimal, put the number of parts in the first place after the point. For example, 4/10 = 0.4. Say 'four tenths.'

Try it yourself: five questions

Q1 Practice 1 of 5

Write 2/10 as a decimal.

Answer

0.2.

The bottom number is 10, so put 2 in the tenths place.

Q2 Practice 2 of 5

Write 6/10 as a decimal.

Answer

0.6.

Six out of ten equal parts is six tenths.

Q3 Practice 3 of 5

Write 0.9 as a fraction with 10 on the bottom.

Answer

9/10.

The 9 is in the tenths place. It means nine of ten equal parts.

Q4 Practice 4 of 5

A strip has 10 equal parts. Seven are colored. What decimal of the strip is colored?

Answer

0.7.

Seven colored parts out of ten is 7/10, written 0.7.

Q5 Practice 5 of 5

A strip has 10 equal parts. Two are colored. How many more parts should you color to show 0.4?

Answer

2 more parts.

The decimal 0.4 means four colored parts. Two are already colored, so color two more.

Understanding hundredths

A hundredth is one of 100 equal parts of a whole. Hundredths use two places after the decimal point. For example, 34/100 = 0.34. For a small number such as 6/100, use a zero to hold the tenths place: 0.06.

Try it yourself: five questions

Q6 Practice 1 of 5

Write 12/100 as a decimal.

Answer

0.12.

Twelve hundredths is written with two digits after the decimal point.

Q7 Practice 2 of 5

Write 0.41 as a fraction with 100 on the bottom.

Answer

41/100.

Two decimal places tell us to count hundredths.

Q8 Practice 3 of 5

A grid has 100 equal squares. Eighteen are colored. What decimal of the grid is colored?

Answer

0.18.

Eighteen out of one hundred is 18/100, or 0.18.

Q9 Practice 4 of 5

Write 4/100 as a decimal.

Answer

0.04.

Use zero tenths and four hundredths. The 4 must be in the second place after the point.

Q10 Practice 5 of 5

A grid has 100 equal squares. Color 2 full rows of 10 and 3 extra squares. What decimal of the grid is colored?

Answer

0.23.

Two rows give 20 squares. Three more makes 23 squares out of 100, or 0.23.

Reading and writing decimals

Read the whole number first. Then say 'and' and name the decimal part. One digit after the point names tenths; two digits name hundredths. For example, 1.36 is 'one and thirty-six hundredths.' In 1.06, the zero keeps the 6 in the hundredths place.

Try it yourself: five questions

Q11 Practice 1 of 5

Write 0.2 in words.

Answer

Two tenths.

There is one digit after the decimal point, so its place is tenths.

Q12 Practice 2 of 5

Write 0.43 in words.

Answer

Forty-three hundredths.

Read 43 together, then name the last place: hundredths.

Q13 Practice 3 of 5

Write 'one and six tenths' as a decimal.

Answer

1.6.

Write 1 before the decimal point and 6 in the tenths place.

Q14 Practice 4 of 5

Write 'three and eight hundredths' as a decimal.

Answer

3.08.

Write 3 wholes, zero tenths, and 8 hundredths.

Q15 Practice 5 of 5

In 4.26, what is the value of the 2? What is the value of the 6?

Answer

2 tenths = 0.2; 6 hundredths = 0.06.

The first place after the point is tenths. The second is hundredths.

Equivalent decimals

Equivalent decimals look different but name the same amount. A zero added at the end of the decimal part does not change the value: 0.4 = 0.40. Four tenths and forty hundredths cover the same amount of a whole. Do not move the zero in front of the 4: 0.04 is only four hundredths.

Try it yourself: five questions

Q16 Practice 1 of 5

Complete the matching decimal: 0.2 = 0.__.

Answer

0.20.

Two tenths equals twenty hundredths. Fill the two blanks with 20.

Q17 Practice 2 of 5

Write 0.60 using just one digit after the decimal point.

Answer

0.6.

Removing the final zero leaves the value unchanged.

Q18 Practice 3 of 5

Choose the decimal equal to 0.9: 0.09 or 0.90.

Answer

0.90.

Nine tenths equals ninety hundredths, not nine hundredths.

Q19 Practice 4 of 5

Write 1.4 with two digits after the decimal point.

Answer

1.40.

Add a zero after the 4. One whole and four tenths is still the same amount.

Q20 Practice 5 of 5

Are 0.7 and 0.07 equal? Explain using tenths and hundredths.

Answer

No. 0.7 is seven tenths; 0.07 is seven hundredths.

Write 0.7 as 0.70. Seventy hundredths is not the same as seven hundredths.

Comparing decimals

Compare whole numbers first, then tenths, then hundredths. Use < for less than, > for greater than, and = for equal to. You may add a zero at the end to help: 0.6 = 0.60. To compare 0.6 and 0.48, compare 60 hundredths and 48 hundredths. The greater amount is 0.6.

Try it yourself: five questions

Q21 Practice 1 of 5

Which is greater: 0.2 or 0.6?

Answer

0.6.

Six tenths is greater than two tenths.

Q22 Practice 2 of 5

Fill the blank with <, >, or =: 0.31 __ 0.38.

Answer

0.31 < 0.38.

Both have three tenths. One hundredth is less than eight hundredths.

Q23 Practice 3 of 5

Fill the blank with <, >, or =: 0.4 __ 0.40.

Answer

0.4 = 0.40.

Four tenths equals forty hundredths.

Q24 Practice 4 of 5

Which is greater: 0.7 or 0.58?

Answer

0.7.

Write 0.7 as 0.70. Seventy hundredths is greater than fifty-eight hundredths.

Q25 Practice 5 of 5

Put these decimals in order from least to greatest: 0.9, 0.24, 0.6.

Answer

0.24, 0.6, 0.9.

Use hundredths: 24, 60, 90. The smallest is 24 and the largest is 90.

Decimals in everyday life

Decimals help us describe money and measurements. One dollar is 100 cents, so 32 cents is $0.32. One meter is 100 centimeters, so 46 centimeters is 0.46 meter. When a bottle label says 0.8 liter, read 'eight tenths of a liter.' Keep the unit with your answer. You do not need to convert milliliters in this practice.

Try it yourself: five questions

Q26 Practice 1 of 5

A sticker costs 24 cents. Write its price in dollars.

Answer

$0.24.

Each cent is one hundredth of a dollar. Twenty-four cents is twenty-four hundredths of a dollar.

Q27 Practice 2 of 5

A pencil costs $0.63. How many cents is that?

Answer

63 cents.

The two digits after the decimal point show cents.

Q28 Practice 3 of 5

A ribbon is 42 centimeters long. Use 100 centimeters = 1 meter to write its length in meters.

Answer

0.42 meter.

Forty-two centimeters is 42/100 of a meter.

Q29 Practice 4 of 5

A bottle is labeled 0.6 liter. Read the amount in words.

Answer

Six tenths of a liter.

The 6 is in the tenths place. Keep the unit, liter.

Q30 Practice 5 of 5

One eraser costs $0.4 and another costs $0.40. Do they cost the same amount?

Answer

Yes. Both cost 40 cents.

Four tenths of a dollar equals forty hundredths of a dollar.

Quick questions and answers

What are tenths and hundredths?

A tenth is one of ten equal parts of a whole. A hundredth is one of one hundred equal parts. The first place after a decimal point is tenths; the second is hundredths.

How do I write four hundredths?

Write 0.04. The zero holds the tenths place, so the 4 stays in the hundredths place. The number 0.4 would mean four tenths instead.

Why are 0.6 and 0.60 equal?

Six tenths and sixty hundredths name the same amount. Adding a zero at the end of the decimal part does not change that amount.

How can I compare decimals?

Compare the whole-number parts, then tenths, then hundredths. Add an ending zero when helpful: 0.7 = 0.70, so 0.7 is greater than 0.58.
