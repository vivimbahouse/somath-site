Understanding SHSAT Question 58 from the College Board

SHSAT Question 58 by SOMATH: A Detailed Analysis

📘 Question (SHSAT-style)

58. Simplify:

−3.8+2.3−(−1.1)

✅ Step-by-Step Solution

−3.8+2.3−(−1.1)

First, remember a key rule: subtracting a negative is the same as adding a positive

=−3.8+2.3+1.1

🎯 Final Answer

−0.4

🧠 SHSAT Insight (Very Important)

This type of problem is classic SHSAT material.

Even though it looks simple, it tests three critical skills:

  • understanding operations with negative numbers
  • applying order of operations
  • avoiding common mistakes with signs

👉 Many students lose easy points here by rushing or misreading “minus a negative.”
