If you invest in local, differentiated education businesses — or if you are a parent who wants to understand how seriously we are building the neighborhood math school your children deserve — the story is on the following ten pages. Every claim is grounded in a specific asset we have already put in place.

1. A visible Upper West Side home, built to be found

SOMATH is not a pop-up. The school occupies a ground-floor storefront at 226 W 79th Street — the kind of visible, walkable Manhattan location where relationships with families are built by being seen every day, not by paid ads. The sign, the classroom, and the sales office are already open.

2. Transit at the door, an affluent education market at scale

The building sits at the 1-train stop on 79th Street/Broadway, with the 2/3 express two blocks away. Within the ZIP code:

  • 6,599 residents ages 5–17
  • 6,541 households with children under 18
  • $181,600 median household income

That is a rare combination in the U.S.: dense, walkable, high-earning, and packed with school-age children who already commute past the door.

3. Ten feeder schools within walking distance

Neighborhood tutoring lives or dies on proximity to schools. Within a short walk of SOMATH:

  • NYC public: P.S. 87, The Anderson School, M.S. 245 The Computer School, M.S. 247 Dual Language, P.S. 9 Sarah Anderson, M.S. 243 Center School
  • Independent: Rodeph Sholom, The Calhoun School, Dwight, Trinity

That is a concentrated feeder market for enrichment, Regents, SHSAT, SAT and AP support — the exact programs SOMATH runs.

4. A full seven-day schedule and a recurring-revenue tuition model

SOMATH runs a full weekly grid: 23 class meetings across weekdays 3:15–9:00 PM and weekends 10:00 AM–5:00 PM. Families pick one weekly class and stay enrolled through a simple month-to-month membership — no annual contract — charged every 30 days.

Monthly tuition (4 sessions, 1 class/week)

  • Little Newtons — $246
  • Kid Einsteins — $367
  • Young Fermats / Regents — $489
  • SHSAT Prep — $587
  • SAT Prep — $640
  • AP Courses — $800

Plus a $99 one-time enrollment fee. Start any week, cancel anytime.

The model is intentionally SaaS-like: predictable monthly cash flow, low churn friction, and a natural expansion path as students climb the SOMATH curriculum ladder.

5. $1.2M classroom run rate at 75% occupancy

The physical capacity is already there. 2 classrooms × 8 students × 23 weekly slots = 368 membership seats per month. At an average tuition of $367:

  • 25% occupancy — $33,764 / month
  • 50% occupancy — $67,528 / month
  • 75% occupancy — $101,292 / month, or $1,215,504 annually
  • 100% occupancy — $135,056 / month

Add the private tutoring room ($120/hour × 20 billable hours × 4.33 weeks = $10,392/month) and the combined base case at 75% occupancy is $111,684 per month, $1,340,208 annualized. This model excludes the $99 enrollment fees — they are extra.

6. Known operating expenses total about $22K/month

Costs are simple and disclosed. Rent, power, marketing, secretary/sales, and two teachers total $22,008/month ($264,097 annualized). Payroll is $15,458 — about 70% of the known base — and covers seven-day operations at $40/hour for teachers and $25/hour for the secretary/sales role.

The number families and investors care about most: break-even is roughly 60 memberships — about 16% of the 368-seat capacity. Below every occupancy tier from slide 5 is a profit.

7. #1 organic position for "math school" — reached in two months

Distribution is often the missing piece for local education businesses. SOMATH built its own. A Perplexity-led SEO and content program moved the school into the #1 organic Google Business Profile position for "math school" in the local Upper West Side pack, against a 5,400 monthly search volume, with 100% visibility in the tracked keyword. Google Business Profile rating: 5.0 stars across 11 reviews.

That means most of the enrollment funnel is already unpaid — a structural advantage that lowers customer acquisition cost and compounds every month new content ships.

8. A Zoom engine that adds ~$298K without new real estate

The Upper West Side classroom is the flagship — but the SOMATH curriculum, teachers and brand are not geographically limited. The second engine is Zoom.

A five-cohort online launch — Algebra I, Algebra II, Geometry & Trigonometry, Regents Prep and AP/Honors — with 12 online seats per cohort = 60 additional memberships. At a $551 weighted average monthly tuition (four cohorts at $489, one at $800), the base case at 75% occupancy is:

  • 50% online occupancy — $16,536 / month
  • 75% online occupancy — $24,804 / month, or $297,648 annually
  • 100% online occupancy — $33,072 / month

No additional classroom, no additional rent, no additional local hire required to unlock that revenue.

9. SOMATH wins on intimacy; the legacy competitor wins on scale

The nearest reference point is RSM Upper West Side, 237 W 72nd Street. RSM is a nearly 30-year-old chain with a 250K+ claimed alumni network and a competition pathway — real strengths. Its weaknesses are the mirror image of ours: quote-required pricing, an average class of 12 students, longer weekly commitment, and a 3.4-star / 20-review local rating.

SOMATH sits in a different lane, on purpose:

  • Small cohorts of up to 8 students
  • Flexible monthly membership, published pricing $246–$800
  • Broad Regents, AP, SAT & SHSAT support in one place
  • 5.0 Google rating across 11 reviews
