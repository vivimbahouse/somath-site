The Test

SHSAT structure: what your child actually sits through

The Specialized High Schools Admissions Test is a single 180 minute exam that decides admission to eight of NYC's nine specialized high schools — Stuyvesant, Bronx Science, Brooklyn Tech, Brooklyn Latin, HSMSE, HSAS, Queens HSS, and Staten Island Tech. (LaGuardia auditions separately.) You get one test, one score, and roughly one hour to think per section.

What's on it

SectionQuestionsTimeWhat it measures
Math57 total (52 grid‑in or multiple choice + 5 field‑test)~90 min sharedPre‑algebra, algebra I, geometry, ratios, number theory, probability, coordinate geometry — no calculator.
ELA57 total (revising/editing + reading comprehension)~90 min sharedGrammar, sentence logic, reading passages with inference and evidence questions.

Key rule: the 180 minutes are shared — students can spend more time on math and less on ELA (or vice versa). Pacing strategy matters as much as content mastery.

FAQ

SHSAT prep, answered honestly

How does SHSAT membership work?

Same monthly membership as the rest of SOMATH — $587/month for the SHSAT Prep tier (8 sessions, 120 min each). Start any week, pause up to 4 weeks/year, cancel with 15 days’ notice. The one nuance: most families stay enrolled through the October test, then either continue with a different track or step away.

When should my child start?

For 7th graders, January–June of 7th grade is ideal. For 8th graders, the latest realistic start is June before the October test. Earlier is better — SHSAT math rewards 6–9 months of accumulated reps, not cram sessions.

What's the difference between SHSAT Prep and Young Fermats Algebra Ignite?

Algebra Ignite covers algebra content. SHSAT Prep covers the test — question types, timing, trap patterns, scratch-paper strategy, and the four question families NYCDOE reuses every year. Strong students often do both: Algebra Ignite for content depth, SHSAT for test-specific tactics.

Do you offer 1-on-1 SHSAT tutoring?

Yes, on request after the free evaluation. Most students do well in the small-group cohort and don't need it. We'll be honest about whether 1-on-1 is necessary based on the diagnostic.

What about ELA?

SOMATH is a math school. We brief students on ELA test mechanics in the final week, but we don't teach the ELA section. If your child needs ELA prep, we can refer you to colleagues we trust.
