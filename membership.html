Membership FAQ

Do I have to wait for a semester start?

No. SOMATH Membership lets your child start any week of the year. Pick a tier, book the free 60-minute evaluation, and we slot them into the right cohort the following week.

How does cancellation actually work?

Cancel anytime with 15 days' written notice (email is fine). No early-termination fee. No contract lock-in. No hidden charges. You're billed for the 15 days remaining, then it stops.

Can we pause for vacations or summer travel?

Yes. Every membership includes up to 4 weeks of free pause per year. Use it for travel, illness, or a heavy school stretch. Just give us 7 days' notice and we hold your seat.

What's the difference between membership and the 16-week term plan?

Same instructors, same classrooms, same curriculum. The term plan is for families who want to commit upfront and save 15% by prepaying the full Fall, Spring, or Summer. Membership is for families who want flexibility — start any week, cancel anytime, pause when needed. Pick whichever fits how you actually plan your year.

What's included in the $99 registration?

A 60-minute placement test with a SOMATH lead instructor, a written diagnostic delivered within 48 hours, and a personalized learning plan that's yours to keep. Charged once per child, not per term.

What if my child misses a class?

Each membership includes one make-up class per month, scheduled into another cohort at the same level. Email us at least 24 hours ahead and we'll find the slot.

Is there a sibling discount?

Yes — $30 off per month for each additional child on any membership tier. Applied automatically at checkout.

Are classes in-person or online?

All membership classes are in-person at our Upper West Side location, 226 W 79th St (1st floor). We don't do online tutoring — small-room teaching with whiteboards is the SOMATH method.
