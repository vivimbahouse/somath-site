05 · FAQ

Questions UWS parents ask before they enroll.

If you don't see your question, give us a call at (646) 668-6151 — we answer the phone.

How is SOMATH different from Russian Math, Kumon, or Mathnasium?

We're not a chain. SOMATH is a small math school founded by two educators who've spent twenty years preparing students for MIT, Harvard, and Stanford. Classes are small, taught live by Harvard- and Northwestern-trained teachers, and built around understanding — not worksheet drills.

What ages and grades do you teach?

Grades 1 through 12 — from Little Newtons (ages 6–10) through SHSAT prep in middle school and AP / SAT prep in high school. Every student starts with a free 60-minute evaluation so we can recommend the right track.

What does the free evaluation actually look like?

It's 60 minutes, one-on-one with a SOMATH teacher, at our Upper West Side location or on Zoom. We assess where your child is, what gaps to close, and which track they belong in. You'll get a written summary — even if you don't enroll.

How long are SOMATH programs?

Our core programs run for 5 months in structured semesters — long enough to actually move the needle, short enough to keep the work focused.

Do you prep for the SHSAT, SAT, and APs?

Yes. SHSAT prep is a dedicated middle-school track (grades 7–8). For high schoolers we run AP Pre-Calculus, AP Statistics, AP Calculus AB / BC, and SAT math prep. Most of our SHSAT students see 30+ point gains; many earn seats at Bronx Science, Stuyvesant, or Brooklyn Tech.

Where are you located?

226 West 79th Street on the Upper West Side — between Broadway and Amsterdam, two blocks from the 79th Street station on the 1, and a short walk from the 2, B, and C lines.
