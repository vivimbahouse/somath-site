Little Newtons

5-month semester · Small cohorts

The years that decide whether a kid grows up loving math or fearing it. Little Newtons is built for elementary students who are ready to go beyond the worksheet — and for the ones who just need someone to slow down and explain.

We focus on number sense, fractions, and word-problem reasoning. The goal isn't to push kids ahead by a year; it's to make sure their foundation is so solid that everything built on it — middle school Algebra, Geometry, the SHSAT — has somewhere stable to land.

  • Number sense & place value
  • Fractions, decimals, percents
  • Word-problem strategy
  • Multiplication fluency
  • Patterns & early algebraic thinking
  • Confidence with math anxiety
