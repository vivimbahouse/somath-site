First and second grade are the years that decide whether a child grows up confident with numbers or quietly convinced that math “isn’t for them.” Little Newtons is built for these foundational learners who are ready to think, not just fill in worksheets.

We focus on number sense up to 100, addition and subtraction fluency, an honest first look at fractions and measurement, and the language of word problems. The goal isn’t to race ahead a grade; it’s to make the foundation strong enough that Kid Einsteins, Young Fermats, and eventually the SHSAT have somewhere stable to land.

  • Number sense & place value to 100+
  • Addition & subtraction fluency
  • Introduction to fractions & measurement
  • Shapes, geometry & spatial reasoning
  • Word-problem language & strategy
  • Patterns & early algebraic thinking

The upper-elementary years — 3rd through 5th grade — are where math either clicks or quietly slips away. Kid Einsteins is built for students ready to move past basic arithmetic into real problem-solving.
