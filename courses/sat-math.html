What students learn

A typical class

Sample class format · 120 minutes

Every SAT Math class follows the same structure so students walk in knowing what to expect and walk out with a corrected mini-quiz in hand.

0:00 – 0:30
30 min

Puzzles & Enigmas

Warm-up with logic puzzles, math riddles, and short brain-teaser problems. The point is to switch the brain on without yet introducing today’s topic — students arrive at different focus levels and this 30-minute block evens the room.

0:30 – 1:00
30 min

Subject — concept & guided practice

First half of today’s topic. One Digital SAT topic per meeting — linear systems, advanced algebra, problem-solving and data, or additional topics in geometry/trig. Concept-first: the instructor demonstrates one or two worked examples, then students try guided practice with the teacher walking the table.

1:00 – 1:05
5 min

Brain break

A short break — water, stretch, a quick chat. Five minutes is enough to reset focus without losing the rhythm of the class.

1:05 – 1:30
25 min

Subject — word problems & applications

Second half of the topic, focused on word problems, abstraction, and math applied to real situations — the place where rote knowledge turns into real understanding. Examples we use: Reading a real Digital SAT-style data set and computing the correct statistic under timing.; Modeling exponential growth in a savings account question.; Decoding wordy SAT geometry problems — strip the words, keep the picture..

1:30 – 2:00
30 min

Mini-quiz — corrected in class

A short timed mini-quiz on today’s topic, then we correct it together — question by question, out loud, with the right strategy spelled out for each item. Parents get the corrected paper at the end of the session so they know exactly where their child stands.

Syllabus arc

24-class rolling syllabus

Courses are rolling — a student can join at any class and continue without losing the thread. Here’s the full topic arc:

Class1Linear equations and inequalities — SAT pacing.
Class2Systems of equations — substitution and elimination on the SAT.
Class3Ratios, rates, proportions — Problem Solving & Data.
Class4Percent problems and percent change.
Class5Data analysis — tables, scatterplots, and best-fit lines.
Class6Statistics — mean, median, mode, standard deviation.
Class7Exponents and exponential functions.
Class8Polynomials and factoring.
Class9Quadratic functions — vertex, intercepts, and discriminant.
Class10Rational expressions and equations.
Class11Function notation, composition, and transformations.
Class12Geometry — circles, triangles, area.
Class13Right triangle trigonometry on the SAT.
Class14Complex numbers and additional topics.
Class15Section 1 of the Digital SAT — full timed run.
Class16Section 2 (adaptive) — strategy and timed run.
Class17Heart of Algebra — full module under timed conditions.
Class18Problem Solving & Data Analysis — full module under timed conditions.
Class19Passport to Advanced Math — full module under timed conditions.
Class20Additional Topics — geometry, trigonometry, complex numbers.
Class21Full-length Digital SAT practice #1 — adaptive routing analyzed.
Class22Digital SAT — advanced quadratic and rational-function modeling problems.
Class23Full-length Digital SAT practice #2 — adaptive routing analyzed.
Class24Digital SAT — Desmos calculator strategy for graphing, regression, and systems.
