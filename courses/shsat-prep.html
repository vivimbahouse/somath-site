A typical class

Sample class format · 120 minutes

Every SHSAT Prep class follows the same structure so students walk in knowing what to expect and walk out with a corrected mini-quiz in hand.

0:00 – 0:30
30 min

Puzzles & Enigmas

Warm-up with logic puzzles, math riddles, and short brain-teaser problems. The point is to switch the brain on without yet introducing today’s topic — students arrive at different focus levels and this 30-minute block evens the room.

0:30 – 1:00
30 min

Subject — concept & guided practice

First half of today’s topic. One of the 16 SHSAT topic areas per meeting — arithmetic, algebra, geometry, or number theory under timed conditions. Concept-first: the instructor demonstrates one or two worked examples, then students try guided practice with the teacher walking the table.

1:00 – 1:05
5 min

Brain break

A short break — water, stretch, a quick chat. Five minutes is enough to reset focus without losing the rhythm of the class.

1:05 – 1:30
25 min

Subject — word problems & applications

Second half of the topic, focused on word problems, abstraction, and math applied to real situations — the place where rote knowledge turns into real understanding. Examples we use: Using fraction shortcuts to skip long calculations on timed problems.; Reading a real SHSAT word problem and identifying the trap before solving.; Allocating 75 seconds per question — building a real pacing rhythm..

1:30 – 2:00
30 min

Mini-quiz — corrected in class

A short timed mini-quiz on today’s topic, then we correct it together — question by question, out loud, with the right strategy spelled out for each item. Parents get the corrected paper at the end of the session so they know exactly where their child stands.

Syllabus arc

24-class rolling syllabus

Courses are rolling — a student can join at any class and continue without losing the thread. Here’s the full topic arc:

Class1Arithmetic foundations — fractions and decimals under SHSAT timing.
Class2Ratio, proportion, and percent — the SHSAT favorites.
Class3Number theory — factors, multiples, GCF/LCM, divisibility, remainders.
Class4Algebra — solving equations and inequalities efficiently.
Class5Algebra word problems — translating fast.
Class6Exponents, radicals, and scientific notation.
Class7Functions and patterns — SHSAT-style.
Class8Coordinate geometry — slope, midpoint, distance.
Class9Geometry — angles, triangles, and Pythagorean.
Class10Geometry — area, perimeter, and circles.
Class11Volume and surface area on the SHSAT.
Class12Probability and counting principles.
Class13Data analysis — tables, charts, and averages.
Class14Logic and reasoning questions.
Class15Full timed math section — 57 questions in 75 minutes.
Class16SHSAT trick patterns — the four question families head-to-head.
Class17SHSAT math — full timed section under test conditions.
Class18SHSAT math review — error patterns from Mtg 17, line-by-line.
Class19SHSAT ELA revising/editing — full timed section.
Class20SHSAT ELA reading comprehension — full timed passage set.
Class21Full-length SHSAT practice test #1 — timed, in our classroom.
Class22Practice test #1 review — every wrong answer reconstructed.
Class23Full-length SHSAT practice test #2 — timed, in our classroom.
Class24Test-day plan — pacing, snack, sleep, and final question-family review.
