About the course

What students learn

In-person AMC 8, AMC 10 and AMC 12 preparation for grades 6–12 on the Upper West Side. Build creative problem-solving in number theory, algebra, geometry, counting and probability, with practice matched to readiness.

One team. Different starting points.

Our initial focus is AMC 8, AMC 10 and AMC 12. Students learn to spot structure, test small cases, draw useful diagrams and explain why a solution works. A sixth grader building number sense and a senior tackling advanced algebra will receive different problem sets. The free evaluation guides placement and extension work.

Choose the right competition pathway

  • AMC 8 foundations: arithmetic, proportional reasoning, number theory, geometry, counting and probability for eligible students.
  • AMC 10 development: deeper algebra, geometry, number theory and combinatorics for ready students.
  • AMC 12 extensions: advanced high-school algebra and trigonometry for students with the prerequisites. Calculus is not the focus.

The MAA sets AMC eligibility: AMC 8 is for grade 8 and below, AMC 10 for grade 10 and below, and AMC 12 for grade 12 and below, with additional age limits. These are eligibility ceilings, not required starting grades.

Beyond the AMC

When useful for a student's goals, we include MATHCOUNTS-style preparation for eligible grades 6–8 students, Math Kangaroo puzzles, school math leagues and age-appropriate olympiad-style challenges. The aim is flexible mathematical thinking, not collecting contest names.

SOMATH provides independent preparation. Contest eligibility, registration, entry fees and participation are separate and governed by each organizer. Enrollment does not guarantee contest admission, qualification or awards.

Not sure where to begin? Book a free evaluation or review the weekly schedule. Try our AMC practice resources in the Journal.

A typical class

Sample class format · 120 minutes

Every Tao Titans · Math Competition Prep class follows the same structure so students walk in knowing what to expect and walk out with a corrected mini-quiz in hand.

0:00 – 0:30
30 min

Puzzles & Enigmas

Warm-up with logic puzzles, math riddles, and short brain-teaser problems. The point is to switch the brain on without yet introducing today’s topic — students arrive at different focus levels and this 30-minute block evens the room.

0:30 – 1:00
30 min

Subject — concept & guided practice

A shared problem-solving idea, demonstrated with accessible examples. Students then try questions at their own level while the teacher checks their reasoning.

1:00 – 1:05
5 min

Brain break

A short break — water, stretch, a quick chat. Five minutes is enough to reset focus without losing the rhythm of the class.

1:05 – 1:30
25 min

Subject — word problems & applications

Level-specific AMC practice and selected competition challenges. Students compare approaches, investigate tempting wrong turns, and learn when a diagram, an equation or systematic casework is the most efficient tool.

1:30 – 2:00
30 min

Mini-quiz — corrected in class

A short timed mini-quiz on today’s topic, then we correct it together — question by question, out loud, with the right strategy spelled out for each item. Parents get the corrected paper at the end of the session so they know exactly where their child stands.

Frequently asked

Common questions from parents

Who is Tao Titans for?

Students in grades 6–12 who enjoy mathematical challenges and want to prepare for competitions. A free evaluation helps us choose an appropriate starting level; previous competition experience is not required.

Which competitions are the main focus?

Our initial focus is AMC 8, AMC 10 and AMC 12. We also use suitable MATHCOUNTS, Math Kangaroo, school math league and olympiad-style problems to develop flexible reasoning.

Will everyone solve the same questions?

No. Shared problem-solving themes are paired with level-appropriate practice. AMC 8 foundations and advanced AMC 10 or AMC 12 extensions are assigned according to readiness, not just school grade.

Which AMC is my child eligible for?

AMC 8 is for grade 8 and below, AMC 10 for grade 10 and below, and AMC 12 for grade 12 and below. Each has an additional age limit. We discuss suitable preparation during evaluation; families should confirm current eligibility and registration with the organizer.

When and where does the class meet?

Fridays from 4:00–6:00 PM at School of Math, 226 W 79th St, 1st Floor, Upper West Side, New York. Classes are in person, with a maximum of six students.

What does membership cost?

Tuition is $587 per month, plus a $99 one-time registration fee. Course materials are included. Competition registration and entry fees are separate; membership does not automatically enter a student in any contest.

Is SOMATH an official contest organizer?

This is independent competition preparation. No official affiliation, contest entry, qualification or award is promised. Contest organizers set their own eligibility, registration rules and deadlines.
