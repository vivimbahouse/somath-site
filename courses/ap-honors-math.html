A typical class

Sample class format · 120 minutes

Every AP & Honors class at SOMATH follows the same block structure so students know exactly what to expect:

  • 0:00 – 0:30 · Puzzles & Enigmas — warm-up with logic puzzles that switch the brain on before we open a textbook.
  • 0:30 – 1:00 · Concept & guided practice — the teacher demonstrates the day's topic and students work guided problems.
  • 1:00 – 1:05 · Brain break — water, stretch, quick reset.
  • 1:05 – 1:30 · Word problems & applications — where technique turns into understanding.
  • 1:30 – 2:00 · Mini-quiz corrected in class — same-day feedback, sent home to parents.
