Little Newtons

GoalLock in number sense, basic operations and simple problem-solving — with the focus on fluency and confidence, not just “doing the math.”
Duration24 h (16 weeks, 1 class/week × 1.5 h)
LevelChildren entering Kindergarten through 2nd grade
Weekly schedule
WeekMain focus
1Number sense up to 100. Counting, grouping in tens, comparing, ordering, simple number patterns. Activities with blocks, charts, drawings.
2Addition and subtraction up to 20 (with meaning). Strategies: counting up from a number, using 10 as an anchor, “gain/lose” story problems, hands-on manipulatives.
3Addition and subtraction up to 100 (easy). No column work, round tens; number line, story problems, quick mental-math drills.
4Geometry and measurement for grades 1–2. 2D and 3D shapes, simple symmetry, comparing size, weight, time (days, hours, calendar). Hands-on activities.
5Story problems and review. Combine every idea: addition/subtraction problems, measurement, time. Final “Math Olympics 1–2” game with mini-challenges.

Frequently asked

Do I need to apply, or can I just enroll?

Every new family books a free 60-minute evaluation first. We use the time to meet the student, run a quick written diagnostic, and decide together which course is the right fit. You receive the diagnostic write-up within 48 hours, even if you don't enroll. Returning families can skip the eval — just call (646) 668-6151.

How is tuition calculated?

All SOMATH courses are billed at our standard $60/hr rate. The Fall term runs 16 weeks, with every track meeting once per week — the full school-year pace. Little Newtons is 90 minutes/session (24 hours total → $1,440). Every other track is 120 minutes/session (32 hours total → $1,920). Payment is upfront, or in 4 monthly installments by request. Credit card adds a 5% processing fee; Zelle and Venmo are accepted without fee.

What if my child misses a session?

Make-up sessions can be arranged with the lead instructor, subject to scheduling capacity. We do not refund missed sessions, but no child loses material — every session has a written summary and home practice the student can complete to stay on pace.

How big are the classes?

Small. Little Newtons caps at 4–6 students; Kid Einsteins and Young Fermats cap at 6–8. Every cohort is small enough that the instructor knows where each student is struggling — the opposite of a 15-kid worksheet room.

What happens when the Fall term ends?

Every Fall cohort rolls directly into the Spring term (January – May 2026/27). Students who finish the Fall term get priority enrollment and a $100 returning-student credit applied toward Spring tuition. We’ll send a re-enrollment link in early December.

Where are you located?

SOMATH is at 226 W 79th St, 1st Floor, NY 10024 — between Broadway and Amsterdam, two blocks from the 79th St 1 train, four blocks from the 86th St B/C. Walkable from PS 87, PS 199, PS 452, PS 9, the Anderson School, and Riverside Park.
